Conditional 3 & Mixed

Conditional type 3 membayangkan hasil berbeda di masa lalu. Mixed conditionals menghubungkan kondisi lampau dengan hasil sekarang, atau keadaan sekarang dengan hasil lampau.

Fokus: unreal past & consequencesWaktu: 15 menit8 soalTarget: 80%

01 · Simple

1. Third conditional

Gunakan untuk kondisi lampau yang tidak terjadi dan hasil lampau yang juga tidak terjadi.

If + past perfect, would have + past participle

If we had left earlier, we would have caught the train.

Faktanya: we didn't leave earlier, so we didn't catch the train. Klausa if dapat diletakkan di depan atau belakang. Jika di depan, gunakan koma.

She would have passed if she had studied.

02 · Intermediate

2. Mixed: masa lalu → keadaan sekarang

Kondisi lampau yang berbeda menghasilkan keadaan sekarang yang berbeda.

If + past perfect, would + base verb

If I had taken that job, I would live abroad now.

If the airline had stored our luggage correctly, we would have our equipment now.

Fakta: I didn't take that job, so I don't live abroad now.

03 · Complex

3. Mixed: keadaan sekarang → masa lalu

Kondisi umum atau keadaan sekarang menjelaskan hasil yang berbeda di masa lalu.

If + past simple, would have + past participle

If he were more careful, he wouldn't have made that mistake.

If the office were better organized, the team wouldn't have missed yesterday's deadline.

Untuk situasi hipotetis, were dapat digunakan dengan semua subjek dalam ragam formal: If I were you...
